How to Play

Fill in the missing words by adding exactly one letter to the word below. Rearrange the letters as needed to make a new word.

  • For example, not could become tone, note, or torn
  • Words can be guessed in any order
  • A letter can repeat — lead could become ladle by adding a second "L"
  • Every word must be a real English word

New puzzles drop daily at 12am EST.

The rules in more detail

  • Each rung is one letter longer than the one below it, and uses all of its letters plus one new letter. For example, cat → cart, and cart → cater.
  • A full ladder might climb air → hair → chair → chairs → cashier → archives.
  • There are three puzzles every day. Easy usually climbs from a 2-letter word to a 5-letter word, Medium usually from a 3-letter word to a 7-letter word, and Hard usually from a 3-letter word up to about 9 letters.
  • Words can go in any order: fill in whichever rung you can see first.
  • Stuck? Each rung allows up to two hints. The first shows the letters in that rung's word; the second shows its first letter. If you're playing timed, each hint adds 3 minutes to your time.

FAQ

How do you play Letter Ladder?

You start with a short word at the bottom and a long word at the top. Fill in each rung in between by adding one letter to the word below and rearranging the letters into a new real word, until you reach the top word.

How is it different from a classic word ladder?

In a classic word ladder you change one letter at a time and every word stays the same length (for example, cat → cot → dot). In Letter Ladder you add a letter at every step and can rearrange the letters, so the words grow as you climb.

How do hints and timing work?

The timer is optional: turn it on or off with the clock at the top right of the game. Each rung allows up to two hints (first its letters, then its first letter), and in timed mode each hint adds 3 minutes. Timed solves can appear on the daily leaderboard.
