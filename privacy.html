Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

You can play Letter Ladder without an account. If you create one, we store your username, password, optional recovery email, and gameplay data such as puzzle progress, completion times, hints, and stats.

Your username and completion time may appear on the leaderboard. Your recovery email is only used for password recovery. We also use basic, anonymized analytics to understand how people use the game.

Your data is stored securely using Google Firebase. We don't sell or share your information with advertisers.

You can remove your recovery email or permanently delete your account and its data at any time from your profile page.
Questions? Contact us.
