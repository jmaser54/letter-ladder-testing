Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

What we collect

You can play Letter Ladder without an account. If you create one, we collect:

  • A username and password
  • An email address, only if you choose to add one (used solely for password recovery)
  • Your gameplay data — puzzle progress, completion times, hints used, and stats like streaks — so your progress can follow you across devices and appear on the leaderboard

We don't collect your real name, location, or any information beyond what's listed above.

How it's used

  • Your username and completion time may appear publicly on the daily leaderboard if you play in timed mode
  • Your recovery email is used only to send you a password-reset link if you request one — it's never shown to other users or used for anything else
  • Basic, anonymized analytics (via Google Tag Manager) help us understand overall traffic and usage patterns — we don't use this to track you individually across other websites

How it's stored

Account and gameplay data is stored using Google Firebase (Authentication and Firestore), a widely-used cloud platform with its own security and encryption standards. We don't sell, rent, or share your data with advertisers or other third parties.

Your choices

  • You can play entirely as a guest, with no account required
  • You can update or remove your recovery email at any time from your profile page
  • You can permanently delete your account and its data at any time from the delete account page

Questions

If you have questions about this policy or your data, you can reach out through the contact page.
